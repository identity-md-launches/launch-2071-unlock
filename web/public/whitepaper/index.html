UNLOCK whitepaper

Version 1.1 · 18 October 2026 · Ethereum mainnet (chain ID 1)

UNLOCK (UNLK) is a fixed-supply token paired with IMD. The official pool applies cumulative selling quotas that expand at permanent USD fully diluted valuation (FDV) milestones. This document explains the deployed rules, their limits and the manual NFT treasury. It promises no returns.

1. Supply and transfers

The fixed supply is 1,000,000,000 UNLK, with 18 decimals, minted once to the launch factory. The policy allocation is 10% (100 million) to native distributor allocations, 87% (870 million) to pool seeding and 3% (30 million) to the recorded launch payer. The factory’s seed rounding can leave pool dust with the payer; this does not create extra eligible credits. There is no subsequent mint.

Ordinary ERC20 transfers are unrestricted. A transfer moves tokens only: it does not create, move or reset eligible credits or the cumulative sales counter. Receiving tokens by transfer alone does not make them sellable through the official pool before full unlock.

2. Eligible acquisitions and selling rights

Each execution account has cumulative eligible acquisitions E and cumulative official-pool sales S. E includes actual UNLK output from official-pool buys and proven allocation credits after synchronization. It is not the account’s current token balance.

Buy credits are pending during their acquisition block. They become mature in a later block; all buys within the same block remain pending together. matureE is E minus those immature buy credits. Verified payer and native allocation synchronization credits do not use the buy-maturity delay.

Before full unlock, with the stored allowance p expressed in basis points (20% = 2,000): 

quota = max(0, floor(matureE × p / 10000) − S)
sellable = min(current UNLK balance, quota)

For example, 1,000 mature eligible tokens and 200 already sold give a quota of zero at 20%. At 30%, the cumulative cap becomes 300, leaving only 100 additional tokens that can be sold, provided the account has that balance. It is not 30% of the remaining balance, and it is not a fresh 300-token allowance.

Claim, attest and sync are separate

  1. Claim: use the native MerkleDistributor claim with the round’s valid recipient, amount and proof. Its round, claim timing, funding and already-claimed checks apply; a claim transfers the allocated tokens.
  2. Attest: the claim-evidence contract validates a certified round and proof against the native distributor’s claimed state. It records evidence, without transferring tokens or independently creating hook credits.
  3. Sync: after activation, syncRewards(recipient) credits only the newly proven difference. Repeating it cannot duplicate credits. Claims made before activation can be attested and synchronized later.

syncPayer() credits the fixed 3% allocation once to the payer recorded by launch provenance. It does not credit the caller, current treasury, founder by assumption or connected visitor. Moving the NFT inventory wallet changes neither payer attribution nor these rules.

The official pool uses the pinned Universal Router’s execution initiator (msgSender()) for E and S. The eventual token recipient can differ from that initiator. The terminal defaults settlement to the execution account and shows its ledger. A sale must fit the quota that was open before its own post-swap observation.

3. The permanent unlock ladder

The initial allowance is 20%. A valid recorded observation opens every crossed gate through the highest reached tier. Earned gates never relock when prices fall or references fail. The final $50 million gate is a permanent global quota bypass for every holder, including transfer-only recipients. It does not promise liquidity or remove fees.

4. What actually records a gate

The deployed oracle combines the official UNLK/IMD pool’s current sqrtPriceX96 with the admitted IMD/USD source, then uses the fixed one-billion supply to calculate USD FDV. The source combines the separate IMD/native-ETH reference pool’s spot price with validated Chainlink ETH/USD. FDV is not circulating market capitalization.

The oracle checks the exact bound pool, token order, fee, factory seed range and liquidity, minimum seed quote capacity, and reference chain/code/decimals. The reference checks its pool’s current liquidity and configured fee. ETH/USD must have a positive valid round, correct decimals, coherent timestamps, an answered round at least as recent as the requested round, age no greater than 90 minutes and an answer no greater than the source’s cap.

A successful official-pool swap calls the hook’s observation after quota accounting. Anyone may also call checkpoint() (or its updateMilestone() alias) after activation. A valid observation persists the highest crossed tier. An invalid oracle reading blocks new gate advancement, while existing rights and within-quota trading remain available.

Viewing a price or crossing a chart line does not record an unlock. There is no TWAP, 48-hour holding requirement, observation waiting window or price-history reconstruction in these mainnet gates. A brief valid spot-price touch can permanently open a tier. Reading state on this site does not send a checkpoint transaction.

Chart interpretation

The Price chart uses only filtered Swap logs of the official UNLK/IMD pool. UNLK is currency0, IMD is currency1 and both have 18 decimals: IMD per UNLK is (sqrtPriceX96 / 2^96)^2. OHLC uses the recorded post-swap spot observations within timestamped UTC buckets. It is not an execution-average chart and cannot reconstruct prices inside a swap.

Volume is the absolute executed Swap-event UNLK and IMD pool legs, excluding the extra treasury component. Empty intervals have no candles; line segments do not bridge no-trade gaps. Block coverage is bounded and explicitly partial. Boundary candles may cover only part of a bucket; the time axis ends at the scanned block timestamp. Recent blocks may reorganize. Current USD/FDV is shown separately when valid; historical USD candles are unavailable because there is no time-aligned IMD/USD conversion series. Today’s conversion is never applied to historical prices.

5. Pool and treasury fees

The official pool’s LP fee is 1.25% (12,500 pips, denominator 1,000,000) on the input side. Pool LP collections follow the launch’s 80% payer / 20% IMD policy, nominally 1% / 0.25% before other charges. The independent hook treasury component is 0.75% of the executed IMD pool leg, charged in IMD. The two bases differ, so an exact combined 2% claim is incorrect.

Let f(b) = floor(b × 75 / 10000), with amounts in raw 18-decimal IMD units. The four standard modes use these rules:

Hook treasury fee, separate from the pool fee
Mode Treasury calculation
Exact-input buy For gross IMD budget G, choose the largest pool input b with b + f(b) ≤ G. Treasury receives G − b.
Exact-output buy Given actual executed IMD pool input b, treasury receives f(b). Trader pays b + f(b).
Exact-input sell Given actual gross IMD pool output b, treasury receives f(b). Trader receives b − f(b).
Exact-output sell For requested net IMD N, choose the smallest gross pool output b with b − f(b) ≥ N. Treasury receives b − N.

When IMD is the specified side, the fee is fixed before execution and complete fills are required. Its rounding favors the treasury by at most one wei versus floor-based 75 bps. Otherwise the fee is floored on the executed IMD leg after the swap. Pool/core fee arithmetic has its own integer rounding. At zero protocol fee, ignoring price impact and rounding, effective combined costs are about 1.98511166% on buys and 1.990625% on sells.

Uniswap core can charge a directional protocol fee in addition to the LP fee. Core compounds the two: protocol + LP − floor(protocol × LP / 1,000,000), in pips. Quotes read the current directional fee; price impact, slippage, rounding and ETH gas are separate. Fees continue after full unlock. ETH is used for gas, not as the trading pair.

6. Vault, treasury and NFT custody

The hook accrues PoolManager IMD claims to the vault. The cash path is:

  1. PoolManager claims: accrued fee claims await redemption.
  2. redeem() into the vault: anyone may redeem genuine pending fee claims. The vault burns those claims and receives the IMD itself.
  3. Beneficiary withdraw(): only 0x48e4e74fe1d0c14404ceb57b9fec5b21329c6ec9 may withdraw redeemed fees, and payment goes to that same immutable beneficiary.
  4. Manual transfer to treasury: moving funds onward to 0xFe44fC8F6C32f681a0ce443902a0b08bB5B3C736 requires a separate manual transfer controlled by the beneficiary.

The vault cannot pay the new treasury wallet directly. Redemption does not withdraw funds. The owner reports redeeming prior claims and intends to keep funds in the vault; live chain reads determine the actual amounts. This website update authorizes and performs no fund movements. There is no requirement to withdraw redeemed funds.

The ledger separates lifetime accrued fees, pending claims, lifetime redeemed fees, lifetime withdrawals, currently claimable redeemed fees, actual vault balances, donations and treasury holdings. Accrued = pending + redeemed; redeemed = withdrawn + claimable. Actual vault IMD = claimable + current donations. Actual PoolManager claims = pending fee claims + donated claims. These are overlapping breakdowns, not values to sum into a larger treasury. The external treasury balance is a separate holding, not proven fee revenue.

The new treasury wallet holds the NFT inventory. Discovery uses paginated public indexing filtered to the official collection 0x0000ec93127baa929e58e97dd0095a2bfb38ec1d; displayed tokens are checked with onchain ownerOf, balanceOf and tokenURI. Images are treated as untrusted metadata. Indexing may lag, pages may be incomplete and ownership snapshots may become stale.

NFT purchases and custody are manual. Holdings alone do not prove fee-funded purchases. There are no automatic purchases or burns, promised returns, holder revenue-sharing rights, buybacks or additional utility commitments in these deployed rules.

7. Limits and risks

  • Other-pool bypass: quotas and the 0.75% treasury fee apply only to the official hooked pool. Unrestricted transfers allow other pools or private trades that bypass both. Such activity does not create official-pool eligible acquisition credits.
  • Spot manipulation: manipulating either the UNLK/IMD pool or the external IMD/ETH reference, including within one transaction, can permanently open gates early. The oracle is not manipulation-resistant.
  • Shared executors: a common contract executor can pool mature credits and sales across users. Recipient-level token ownership does not imply separate quota accounting.
  • Market and custody: liquidity, price, execution, manually held NFTs and the beneficiary’s future choices carry risk. No return, liquidity floor or price guarantee is made.
  • Data availability: public RPCs and indexers can fail, rate-limit, lag or reorganize recent blocks. Unknown data is unavailable, not zero. Wallet signing is required for any transaction; displayed estimates are not execution guarantees.
  • Review limits: agent reviews, source comparisons and automated tests are not a conventional independent security audit. Reviewed ABI/source evidence is not a claim of Etherscan source verification.
