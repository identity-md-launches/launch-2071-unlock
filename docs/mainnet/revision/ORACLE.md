## How every price interval is authenticated

Uniswap documents that [v4 has no built-in oracle](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/v3/concepts/price-oracles). This existing pool has no hook, and one cannot retrofit this launch's hook into its PoolKey. Off-chain Swap logs, indexer answers, keeper signatures and caller-timed spot checkpoints are not inputs to this source.

`BlockState.verify` checks RLP(header) against Ethereum `blockhash(number)`, obtains the state root and timestamp from that header, verifies the manager's account Merkle-Patricia inclusion and runtime hash, then verifies slot0 and active liquidity storage inclusion. The slot is `keccak256(abi.encode(PoolId, uint256(6)))`, with liquidity offset +3, exactly the pinned manager's storage layout. There is no caller-supplied timestamp, price or accepted root. Header/account/slot mutation, wrong path/chain, malformed RLP, trailing proof nodes, duplicates, expired headers and premature confirmations are rejected. All verification code is in ordinary source files and libraries are internal.

The accumulator integrates **block-end tick for the elapsed time until the next produced block**. Ethereum transactions within a block share its timestamp; a same-block excursion restored before block end adds no elapsed weight. Each next block must be submitted in order, even if there was no swap. Consequently an observer cannot omit inconvenient produced blocks or multiply a single sample by an unproven elapsed interval. It is an explicit block-end oracle, not an assumed v4 TWAP. End-of-block manipulation carried into the next block still receives real time weight and remains a threat.

Each header must be **64..256 blocks old** on submission. Sixty-four confirmations reduce short reorg exposure; they are not a consensus finality certificate. Price windows end at the last authenticated block, never at an extrapolated current spot. The read requires the last proven timestamp to be at most **20 minutes** old. At normal 12-second blocks, the 64-block lag is about 12.8 minutes, leaving roughly 7 minutes of delivery slack; slow blocks can fail closed sooner. Proofs of every produced block are still needed. This latency is deliberate and must be evaluated for irreversible rights.

A missing block cannot be skipped while still provable. Once its 256-block horizon has expired, anyone can resume only at exactly head-64; the entire missing interval is marked bad. All observations are retained and no configuration/history reset exists. A full clean 24h must follow before pricing resumes. A timestamp gap over 60 seconds, insufficient depth or changed fee policy also marks the interval bad. Storage inclusion is intentionally strict: a missing/zero-valued liquidity leaf cannot authenticate as positive depth; it stalls submission and triggers stale/gap recovery rather than being invented as healthy. Absent data never receives price weight used by a valid window.

## Fixed safeguards and limits

- Both **2h and 24h** geometric IMD/ETH windows must have complete clean authenticated intervals. The source also waits 24h after creation. It uses the lower price and rejects more than **10%** short/long divergence. Direction is normalized before signed floor division, including negative ticks.
- Every historical interval needs at least **10,000 virtual ETH** of active depth, correct static fee10000, the recorded protocol fee, and coherent sqrtPrice/tick. Current manager code, fee policy and active depth are checked again on each price read. Current spot is used only to reject missing depth, never as the price. Flash/JIT liquidity now cannot repair missing historical depth.
- The 10,000-ETH floor is a **screening limit, not an economic security guarantee**. It is about 16× the observed virtual reserve and therefore rejects the current observed market. Virtual depth can also rise because an attacker buys IMD; it does not prove independent liquidity funding, locked liquidity or unrecoverable attack cost. Narrow liquidity, withdrawals, fee recapture and LP ownership prevent interpreting this constant as a loss bound.
- ETH/USD comes only from pinned canonical Chainlink `0x5f4eC3Df9cbd43714FE2740f5E3616155c5b8419`, with 8 decimals, positive complete round, ordered nonfuture timestamps, <=90m age and a $1M sanity ceiling. Proxy/aggregator governance remains external trust. Neither stablecoin peg nor a nonexistent Chainlink IMD feed is assumed.
- UNLK/IMD still uses the existing hook-checkpointed **2h** TWAP with protected seed principal. Sequential **6h**, final **24h**, **4m** minimum cadence and **10m** maximum valid-sample gap are unchanged. Invalid pricing cannot advance or revoke rights, and swaps/native claims/ordinary transfers remain independent of source liveness. A failed activation is atomic. Final quota bypass stays permanent.

Longer windows and agreement across them do not establish fair value. The synthetic sustained-manipulation test deliberately passes after both windows are held at the manipulated price with adequate synthetic depth. Correlated movement in both IMD/ETH and ETH/USD is likewise not solved by window agreement. This is an executable economic limit, not a claim of attack resistance.

## Operator prerequisites and evidence boundary

A new source deployment, a permissionless proof submitter with reliable block/header/storage-proof access, transaction funding and continuous coverage are required after separate release authorization. No such infrastructure was deployed. `scripts/mainnet/oracle-proof.py` produces unsigned calldata, verifies the RLP header hash locally, checks chain/age and requests exact slots. It does not sign or submit. The contract, not the script or RPC, authenticates the proof. The task RPC later refused historical proof generation with `distance to target block exceeds maximum proof window`; the previously captured proof remained verifiable. `--capture-only` captures near-head data, and `--recorded` prepares that retained data after confirmation without trusting its contents. An operational collector must capture every block before pruning and handle reorged captures. Operators must submit every next produced block, monitor count/last timestamp/bad intervals and rehearse outage recovery. A latest-block-only cron is invalid.

The actual proof measured **1,397,989 execution gas**, excluding calldata/base transaction; approximately 7,200 proofs/day implies about **10.07 billion execution gas/day**, or 10.07 ETH/day at an illustrative 1 gwei, before calldata. This is not a quoted gas price. Sustained mainnet affordability, throughput and gas sponsorship are unresolved operational prerequisites. Storage also grows with observations. A reviewed optimization or different oracle infrastructure may be needed; none is disguised as already deployed.

Real evidence: recorded header/account/storage proofs and mainnet fork anchoring; actual no-depth/no-history venues; actual router round-trip measurements; actual alternative-feed reads. Synthetic evidence: warm/deep 24h intervals, manipulation/outage models and the price redirection used to exercise candidate launch/milestone/router paths. These tests are explicitly separated.
