# Actual pricing evidence and remaining safety work

**No reliable production IMD/USD source is certified. Release is blocked.** No IMD/USD Chainlink feed is assumed. `UnlkImdOracle.fdvUsd()` computes fixed 1B UNLK supply × 2h UNLK/IMD TWAP × `ImdUsdSource.priceUsd()`, all normalized to 18-decimal USD. Both tokens are verified 18 decimals. The source takes no UNLK pool or token, preventing circular UNLK-derived IMD pricing.

`ImdUsdSource` is an actual on-chain reader, not an off-chain price setter. Immutable venue, runtime commitment and harmonic-liquidity minimum are supplied to its reviewed constructor. Every read validates the canonical Uniswap v3 factory's getPool result, IMD/WETH identity, venue runtime, current liquidity, 2h and 24h `observe` accumulators and harmonic liquidity in both windows. It conservatively normalizes tick direction before flooring, rejects >20% short/long USD-price disagreement, and uses the lower window price. It requires 24h after source creation as well as sufficient venue history. ETH/USD uses canonical Chainlink proxy `0x5f4eC3Df9cbd43714FE2740f5E3616155c5b8419`, pinned proxy runtime, 8 decimals, positive completed round, nonfuture ordered timestamps, <=90m freshness and $1M ceiling. The proxy's current aggregator is recorded in `network/feed-fees.json`; Chainlink governance/aggregator replacement remains external trust. No stablecoin peg or IMD dollar peg is assumed.

UNLK/IMD history requires 2h warm-up, <=5m last checkpoint age, locked principal position liquidity >=1e18, nonzero opening quote value, price in the seed range, protocol fee zero and LP fee12500 throughout. Same-timestamp replacement has no elapsed price-time weight. The hook checkpoints before/after official price changes; anyone may checkpoint between trades. Source failures are caught by milestone progression, so swaps/native reward claims remain available subject to normal pool/quota/funding conditions. Activation additionally requires a successful IMD/USD source read; a dead or un-warmed source cannot activate.

## Tests and limits

Tests cover warm-up, freshness, nonpositive/future/incomplete ETH/USD rounds, current and harmonic depth, history outage, 2h/24h divergence, an IMD depeg reducing USD value, invalid-data nonprogression, sequential unchanged milestones, permanent bypass and fee continuation. Tests with synthetic 24h histories also demonstrate that **sustained manipulation of both windows can pass agreement**. This is a documented economic limitation, not a safety claim. The UNLK leg can also be sustained-manipulated economically. Window length, depth gate and agreement alone do not prove a cost greater than the value of rights unlocked.

Required evidence before release: a noncircular deployed oracle venue with authenticated immutable identity, adequate *historical and active* liquidity, continuity/outage analysis, independently justified minimum liquidity and cross-window threshold, a quantified sustained/cross-market manipulation budget against value-at-risk for every tier, ETH/USD governance/freshness assumptions, and independent review of the exact adapter/configuration. The fixture's 1e18 minimum is a test parameter, not a certified production depth choice. No constructor arguments for a production source are supplied because the discovered candidate is unusable. Future launch numbers, owner wallets and keys do not block code; missing oracle safety evidence does block activation/release.
